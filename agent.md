# LogicHQ PropTech – Website Build Specification (agent.md)

## 1. Overview

This document defines the complete requirements for building the official website for the LogicHQ PropTech division.

The website must position LogicHQ PropTech as a premium, enterprise-grade technology partner for real estate businesses.  
It is not a generic agency portfolio.  
It is a high-value PropTech solutions platform.

Target audience:

- Real estate agencies  
- Property developers  
- Property managers  
- Commercial real estate firms  
- Real estate startups  

Core objective of the website:

Convince real estate decision-makers that LogicHQ PropTech can help them:

- Generate more buyers and tenants  
- Manage properties efficiently  
- Automate operations  
- Centralize data  
- Modernize their business  

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

The website must communicate that LogicHQ PropTech is:

- Professional  
- Technical  
- Trustworthy  
- Data-driven  
- Real-estate focused  
- Enterprise ready  

Tone of voice:

- Confident  
- Clear  
- Benefit-focused  
- Non-technical  
- Business-oriented  

Avoid:

- Developer jargon  
- Casual language  
- Overly playful design  
- Generic tech agency wording  

---

## 3. Design Guidelines

### Visual Style

The design must feel:

- Premium  
- Corporate  
- Clean  
- Modern  
- Minimal  

Preferred color palette:

- Primary: Dark blue  
- Secondary: White  
- Accent: Black  
- Highlight: Gold or subtle orange  

Typography:

- Modern sans-serif fonts  
- Strong hierarchy  
- Large bold headings  
- Clean readable body text  

Imagery:

- Modern buildings  
- Real estate professionals  
- Dashboards  
- Maps  
- Property listings  
- Mobile app screens  

No cartoon graphics or playful illustrations.

---

## 4. Website Structure

### Required Pages

1. Home  
2. About Us  
3. Services  
4. Solutions by Industry  
5. Case Studies  
6. Resources / Blog  
7. Contact / Demo  

---

## 5. Homepage Requirements

### Hero Section

Must contain:

- Strong headline  
- Clear value proposition  
- Call to action buttons  
- Professional imagery  

Example structure:

Headline:
“PropTech Solutions for Smarter Real Estate Businesses”

Subheading:
We help real estate companies attract buyers, manage properties, and close deals faster using technology.

CTAs:

- Book a Demo  
- Learn More  

---

### Problems Section

Title:

“Challenges We Solve”

List common pain points:

- Scattered property listings  
- Missed inquiries  
- Slow response times  
- No centralized system  
- Manual processes  
- Poor online presence  
- Lack of data insights  

This section must emotionally connect with real estate business problems.

---

### Solutions Overview

Show main offerings with icons and short descriptions:

- Property Management Platforms  
- Custom Real Estate Websites  
- Mobile Applications  
- AI Chatbots  
- CRM Systems  
- Lead Automation  
- Analytics Dashboards  

Each card must link to detailed services.

---

### Results Section

Show measurable outcomes:

- Faster responses  
- More qualified leads  
- Centralized operations  
- Increased conversions  

Use bold numbers and clear benefits.

---

### Trust Section

Include:

- Client logos  
- Testimonials  
- Technology stack  
- Partner badges  

---
